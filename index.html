Clinical-first design

Designed for providers.
Configured for your specialty.

Every feature is built around the clinical encounter — from intake to discharge — then extended with billing tools that work without a separate system.

Adaptive Patient Chart
9 clinical tabs configured to your specialty — SOAP notes, vitals, prescriptions, labs, imaging, referrals, and problem lists. Show only what your practice needs.
Integrated Billing — No Add-ons
EDI 837 submission, 835 ERA auto-posting, 270/271 eligibility, denial management, and secondary billing — all included. No separate clearinghouse subscription ever.
Practice-Wide Communication
Staff messaging, eFax, and SMS appointment reminders built into the clinical workflow. Your front desk, nurses, and providers share one connected stream — no extra apps.
Nothy — AI Clinical Assistant
Speak naturally during or after the encounter and Nothy generates a complete SOAP note, including assessment and plan. Trained on clinical language — not generic AI.
Telemedicine — Native, Not Bolted On
HIPAA-compliant video visits launch directly from the appointment block. Notes auto-attach to the chart. No extra logins, no third-party plugins, no workflow interruption.
Revenue Intelligence Dashboard
Real-time A/R aging, collections by provider and payer, denial rate trends, and tax-ready financials. Exportable to CSV. Built for administrators who need clarity, not complexity.
